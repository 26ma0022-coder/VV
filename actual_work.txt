Brief of what we did:
1. got back 200 years to know what Dhanbad was part of before Jharkhand:
1826 -- Assam joined Bengal
1836 -- North western provinces, Bengal from the Bengal (border-Bihar)
1905 -- Eastern Bengal province, Assam province and Bengal province from Bengal 
1912 -- Bihar province, Orissa province from Bengal province
2000 -- Jharkhand from Bihar
understood: the importance of searching the word "Manbhum"
references:
- 1877: A Statistical Account Of Bengal - Vol.17 - https://archive.org/details/dli.bengal.10689.2816/page/n13/mode/2up
- 1905: Linguistic Survey Of India Vol V Part Ii - https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.73105/page/n13/mode/2up
Decided to base the further search under "Vanishing Voices of Manbhum"

2. used .txt of 1911 Manbhum from archive.org (a: https://archive.org/stream/manbhum01coup/manbhum01coup_djvu.txt) & analysed using voyant tools
findings:
- a high prevalence of a never heard word - "ghatwal*" - 166 times [2nd word]
- chakran - 12 times [3rd word]
- panchak* (panchak, panchaki)- 1o times [4th word]
- found various words that were not specific to our region Manbhum or chotanagpur: barkandaz, sebundies, saranjami, amils, kistbandi
- Project-scope/region specific words that need more references:
i. dhowras, korkar & khandit,  - 1 time each 3
ii. kami* (5): kamiya (2), kamiyas (1), kami (1), kamihi (1)
*came across a local story from clicking kamdeva (1) - can be studied later
Decided to not find more words, enough references and meaning search in the next step.

3. dhowras done very easily as dhowrahs from 1935 The Calcutta Gazette (b: https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.43938/mode/2up) with meaning of the term: "Dhowrah" means a house used or in-tended to be used by colliery labourers.
*the site was hanging (maybe 'cause the text was big), another source: 1952 Industrial Housing in India had 25 occurrences of this mining related term (c: https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.223666)

Kamin: Lahiri-Dutt's 2006 paper (d: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/314669387_Kamins_Building_the_Empire_Class_Caste_and_Gender_Interface_in_Indian_Collieries) proved the presence of this word; there was also a new 2026 book by author Jyoti Sinha called Struggle and Survival of the Kamins (https://books.google.co.in/books/about/Struggle_and_Survival_of_the_Kamins.html?id=RjDl0QEACAAJ&redir_esc=y) but it was not accessible

Kamia, etc.: got clarity that they are non-mining terms; agrarian debt-bondage term used across Bihar (e: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/342409939_The_Abolition_of_Bonded_Labour_and_Slavery_in_India_From_'Poor_Law'_to_a_Fundamental_Right_-_the_Gradual_Changes_of_an_Institution_1843-1990)


4. while finalising the organisation/meaning/references of words, got across pdfs like (f) https://ia801406.us.archive.org/15/items/in.ernet.dli.2015.123779/2015.123779.Dictionary-Of-Law-And-Usage-And-Guide-To-Legal-Translation_text.pdf to create 1st basic schema of word|meaning|reference
1. मानभुम / Manbhum | The name of the area encompassing Dhanbad, Purulia & Bokaro | a
2. घाटवाली / ghatwali | system where local people held land and revenue right for protecting passes | a
3. धौड़ा / dhowrah | Used in mining regulations to define standardized, company-owned tenement lines built for colliery workers | b, c, a
4. कमीन / Kamin | Colloquial term used across Jharia and Raniganj for female surface and underground coal basket loaders who worked alongside male coal-cutters/miners | d
5. कमिया / Kamiya (Kamauti System) | A landless agricultural laborer bound to a landlord through hereditary debt-bondage. | e, a
6. चाकरान / chakran | plots of land granted rent-free to village servants or local public officials instead of paying them a regular cash salary. | f, a
7. पंचक / panchak | an extra historical tax or fee that local landlords (zamindars) charged tenants on top of their standard land rent. | f, a

5. modified schema to account for the following and writing content
- meaning, active usage span & geographic scope
- cause of obsolescence & archival provenance

6. categorising based on time (>200, >100, >50) and thinking what type/domain the word is; then finally thinking and planning the frontend
